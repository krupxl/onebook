## The wordings to test

**K1, the floor under test.** *Never deliberately kill another person, from birth onwards, unless it is needed to stop them from killing or gravely harming someone.*
- It binds everyone acting on their own authority: private persons, and anyone in charge of a household, workplace or community (masters, parents, husbands, teachers, religious leaders, crowds).
- It protects every person from birth onwards, whatever their faith, status, sex or age: newborns, children, wives, slaves and servants, strangers, unbelievers, heretics, "the wicked".
- The exception covers defending oneself or others against deadly or grave attack.

**What K1 does not settle (context only; record such texts, but they never decide a label):**
- (a) punishment imposed through a court or a ruler, as each tradition's law defines it, including a ruler's order;
- (b) war between communities or states;
- (c) life before birth;
- (d) the end of one's own life (suicide, euthanasia, voluntary death such as Jain sallekhanā);
- (e) animals;
- (f) accidental or unintended killing.

**K0, a lower floor** (assessed now so that the method's lower-floor step needs no new research): *Do not deliberately kill anyone for your own gain, or out of anger, hatred or revenge, unless it is needed to stop them from killing or gravely harming someone.*

**K3, a record wording about war** (not part of the floor; recorded because the candidate list names "commanded destruction in ancient war texts" as a difference): *In war, do not deliberately kill people who are not fighting, such as children, the old, civilians and those who have surrendered.*

## Labelling rules (apply them exactly; they mirror the annex rules of Article I)

1. **Only counted texts decide a label.** Counted = Tier 1, or a Tier 2 text that corpus.md and METHOD.md section 9, decision 4 say the tradition treats as primary (for example the Talmud for Rabbinic Judaism, the authentic Sunnah for Islam, the Great Learning and Doctrine of the Mean, ʿAbdu'l-Bahá's writings; NOT the Mahabharata, NOT the Five Classics, NOT the Pahlavi books, NOT Musonius Rufus). Branch rule: a text accepted by one major branch speaks for that branch only. Unverified texts never decide. Other texts are reported beside the label.
2. **Contradicted.** A counted text accepted by every major branch permits, commands or approves a deliberate killing that the wording forbids, for at least one class of victim. This includes a text that declares such a killing wholly blameless and unpunished because of who the victim is or what the victim did. A lesser or different penalty (blood money, expiation, discretionary punishment, a sin before God) is *not* permission: record it as "graded protection", which limits equal protection but does not contradict K1.
3. **Contradicted in one branch.** As rule 2, but only in texts one major branch accepts. Name the branch, and say whether the other branches' texts were found silent or were not checked.
4. **Reporting is not approving.** A story that reports a killing without approving it, an image, a parable, or a text that only presupposes a practice, is Implied and does not decide, unless the text itself (or God, the founder or the teacher within it) says the act was right.
5. **Courts and war are context only** (exclusions (a) and (b)). But a killing that a counted text lets a private person carry out on their own authority, outside any court or ruler (a zealot, a kinsman, a master, a parent, a husband, a crowd), is *not* covered by the exclusion. If it is disputed within the tradition whether a killing was judicial or private, the label is Unclear (rule 6).
6. **Unclear.** A counted text bears directly on the wording, but its meaning or reach is unresolved: a disputed word; a reading that the tradition's own counted texts, its classical commentators, or its authorised interpreting body narrow; or a hard case such as a killing whose judicial or private character is disputed.
7. **Taught.** A counted text teaches the wording at the Explicit or Clear teaching level. A wider duty (not to kill any living being; not to harm anyone) counts if it reaches everyone K1 protects (METHOD.md section 3, "Wider rules"). A duty owed only to some people (for example only to members of one's own community) is **taught in part**, which is not enough for the whole wording.
8. **Silent.** No counted text either way.
9. **From birth onwards.** Newborns and infants are persons for K1. Texts about life before birth are context only.

Strength ratings (Explicit, Clear teaching, Implied) are as in METHOD.md section 3; only Explicit and Clear teaching count.
