# Changelog

All changes to the Code, the Register and the method are recorded here, newest first. Dates are in ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## 2026-10-13

### Taoism corpus and translations completed (step 2, follow-up)
- Completed the Taoism sections of `sources/corpus.md` and `sources/translations.md`, which had been marked pending.
- Tier 1 unchanged (Tao Te Ching; Zhuangzi), with the Zhuangzi Inner Chapters weighted most and early-manuscript variants recorded. Tier 2 keeps the Ganying Pian and adds the classical commentaries (Heshang Gong, Wang Bi, Guo Xiang), the Liezi (flagged) and religious Taoist precept texts.
- Translation: James Legge (1891), public domain, checked on ctext.org.

### Candidate list (step 4)
- Replaced the `candidates.md` placeholder with 28 candidate principles: the 15 seeds from the brief and 13 further principles that recur across many traditions, found in the first research session.
- Proposed a floor wording for every candidate.
- Split four seeds whose parts are likely to fare differently (protect the vulnerable / the stranger / the guest; anger / humility; fair dealing / just judgement; do not waste / care for the earth), giving 33 test items in all.
- Merged five narrower principles into the candidate they are a case of (honest weights, fair wages, returning trusts, care for the sick, patience), and recorded two that were considered and not listed (keeping confidences; seeking knowledge).
- Flagged belief risks (ego, gratitude to God, sacredness of the earth, envy) and likely contested candidates.
- Recorded unverified research leads for each candidate, clearly marked as leads, not evidence.

### Source corpus and translations (step 2)
- Replaced the placeholders in `sources/corpus.md` and `sources/translations.md`.
- Confirmed the proposed corpus for each tradition and refined it, with reasons. The five changes that alter what can count on its own are: Tattvārtha Sūtra moved to Tier 1 (Jainism); Seneca's moral essays added to Tier 1 (Stoicism); Shia sources added to Tier 2 (Islam); Gleanings moved to Tier 1 (Bahá'í Faith); A Secular Humanist Declaration (1980) moved to Tier 1 (secular humanism).
- Recorded, in advance of any article, where a Tier 2 text is treated as primary by its tradition (Talmud for Rabbinic Judaism; Pahlavi texts for traditionalist Zoroastrians; Great Learning and Doctrine of the Mean; the authentic Sunnah in Islam; ʿAbdu'l-Bahá's writings).
- The Tirukkural is recorded as context only and is not counted for Hinduism, because its religious affiliation is disputed.
- Chose a public-domain or freely accessible translation for every text, and noted where translation choice materially changes meaning.
- The Taoism sections were left marked as pending, because that research had not finished when this step was committed.

### Method written (step 3, drafted while step 2 research runs)
- Replaced the `METHOD.md` placeholder with a public, plain-English version of the brief's Sections 4 to 7: the traditions, the two-tier corpus, the evidence standard, the eight-step test, the Register and the hypothesis.

This records the method; it does not change the brief's rule that only verified citations count.
