### Article II started (candidate C02, "Do not kill the innocent"), method steps 1 to 4
- **Floor under test (K1):** "Never deliberately kill another person, from birth onwards, unless it is needed to stop them from killing or gravely harming someone", binding everyone acting on their own authority, including within households. Left outside it: courts and rulers, war, life before birth, the end of one's own life, animals and accidents. A lower floor (K0: no killing for gain, anger, hatred or revenge) and a record wording on non-combatants in war (K3) were researched at the same time. `candidates.md`: C02 marked Testing.
- **Research, round 1:** one support researcher, one counter-evidence researcher and one independent verifier per tradition, under nine written labelling rules that mirror the annex rules of Article I.
- **Research, round 2:** for each tradition, a direct verifier who read the deciding passages in the reference sites' own published data, then an independent challenger who argued each label was wrong in either direction.
- **New decision on method** (`METHOD.md`, section 9, decision 12): reading a passage in full in a reference site's own published dataset, or in a named open dataset of the same public-domain or authorised translation, counts as `VERIFIED (direct)`. Reason: the sites could not be opened from the working environment; the text read is the same.
- No verdict yet. Next steps are in `HANDOVER.md`.

### Delegated decisions; Article I's verdict; Register entry Q-002 opened
- **Decisions under delegation.** The project lead left all remaining decisions to the project's judgement. Each decision, with its reason, is now recorded in a new section 9 of `METHOD.md` ("Decisions on method made so far", items 1 to 11). None changes the brief's settled decisions, and none changed a tradition's result in Article I. In brief: the tier and branch rules stay as written; a wider duty counts; a branch is a living division with its own accepted texts (Karaite Judaism yes, historical schools no); a Tier 2 text is "treated as primary" only where the tradition places it with its primary scripture (ʿAbdu'l-Bahá yes; the Mahabharata and the Five Classics no); Sunni hadith outside Bukhārī and Muslim count for Sunni Islam when graded sound or good; an article can be admitted only when every counted citation has been read directly; later authorities never outweigh a counted text; a verified ruling of an authorised interpreting body may make a result unclear; "by plain reference" is defined; the five corpus changes made before any article are approved.
- **Decision D2, wording.** Article I's household floor is *no cruelty* (do not injure, torment, starve, overwork or humiliate anyone in your power), with "injure" meaning harm beyond moderate physical correction. Owning people, physical punishment of those in one's care, and sexual relations without consent with someone in one's power go to the Register.
- **Article I, version 0.5: verdict Register (method step 5).** The article is not admitted: Zoroastrianism does not meet the floor in texts every branch accepts (D1), and *no cruelty* is contradicted by counted texts in four traditions (D2). The floor, status box, section E and the decisions section are rewritten to match.
- **New Register entry `register/q-002-households-and-slavery.md`** (version 0.1): the positions of all thirteen traditions, each with what its counted texts say and the position in its own terms; thirty parts (nineteen Textual, eight Logical of which four possibly Axiomatic, three Empirical); five candidate floors, two of them new and untested ("do not punish anyone in your power in anger, or out of proportion to the fault"; "do not seize or sell a free person"); and prioritised next steps. The positions were drafted from the annex by one drafter per tradition, without new searches.
- **Annex, version 0.3.** Records the decided wording and links to Q-002; Islam rows IS-9, IS-12 and IS-17 now count for Sunni Islam (decision 5); HI-7 marked not treated as primary and BA-9 treated as primary (decision 4); 'Abdu'l-Bahá's writings consistently described as treated as primary; rule 6 refers to decision 8 and rule 7 defines "by plain reference"; the humanist result on owning people explains why a freedom clause counts and a common-origin text does not.
- `sources/corpus.md` (version 1.2): definitions of a branch and of "treated as primary"; the rule for Sunni hadith outside the two Ṣaḥīḥs; the approval of the five earlier changes.
- Register Q-001 (version 0.4), `candidates.md` (C01) and `HYPOTHESIS.md` updated: two Register questions open, forty-three parts in all, six possibly axiomatic candidates.

### Households and slavery (decision D2): annex, second independent check, Article I version 0.4
- **New file `code/articles/article-01-annex-households.md`** (version 0.2): the evidence for decision D2 in all thirteen traditions, testing three wordings (no cruelty, no one owned, no hitting). One researcher and one independent verifier per tradition; 191 evidence rows, each with tier, translation, bearing and verification status.
- **Second independent check** (method step 7) of Article I version 0.3 and the annex by five new reviewers. No invented reference was found. The main finding was that results had been labelled by inconsistent standards. In response the annex now has **nine written labelling rules**, applied to every tradition alike, and every result was re-derived under them. Labels that changed: Hinduism L1 and L3, Judaism and Christianity L1 (now "contradicted", as a contradiction for one relationship is a contradiction); Stoicism L1 (now "contradicted", like Exodus 21:20 to 21); Jainism L2 and L3 (now "unclear"); Buddhism L2 and L3 (now "contradicted in one branch", Theravāda); Zoroastrianism L1 ("unclear") and L2 ("contradicted in one branch", after the Denkard Book 8 slave passage was found); Bahá'í Faith L3 ("unclear"). The Torah's captive-woman and spoil texts were added, so that Judaism and Christianity are weighed as Islam is. Further texts found or corrected are listed in the annex's Independent check section.

A lower floor ("do not kill, maim, starve or torment anyone in your power") was tried, as the method requires, and still fails. A Register entry (Q-002) is proposed and waits for the project lead's choice of wording.
